Questions 21-22

Choose TWO letters, A-E.

Which TWO features of the lecture on ocean biodiversity had the greatest impact on the students?

A the references to local problems
B the broad focus of the examples
C the practical suggestions for solutions
D the type of issues discussed
E the implications for government policy

21. First chosen letter:

• Đáp án đúng: B hoặc D
• Dấu hiệu: "focused on more long-term issues" (= D: type of issues discussed) và "examples she gave were at a more global level" (= B: broad focus of the examples).

22. Second chosen letter:

• Đáp án đúng: B hoặc D
• Dấu hiệu: "focused on more long-term issues" (= D) và "examples she gave were at a more global level" (= B).
