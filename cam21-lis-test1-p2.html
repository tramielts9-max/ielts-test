Questions 11-16

Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.

11. What should trainees always expect to get when working on low budget short films?

• Đáp án đúng: A
• Dấu hiệu: "Make sure your transport costs are covered – and remember, there’s very unlikely to be any catering provided, so bring plenty of food". Transport costs = travel expenses.

12. According to the speaker, on big budget films trainees may get experience of

• Đáp án đúng: B
• Dấu hiệu: "But you’re likely to get the opportunity to work with a range of age groups, as well as different ethnicities".

13. The speaker says a problem for makeup artists is

• Đáp án đúng: A
• Dấu hiệu: "working with directors who are impatient, or unhappy with the makeup artist’s work". Impatient directors = difficult directors.

14. How did the speaker feel when she met famous actors for the first time?

• Đáp án đúng: A
• Dấu hiệu: "At first, I found it overwhelming and could hardly speak. I was so in awe". Không dám mở miệng vì quá bẽn lẽn/choáng ngợp = very shy.

15. What advice does the speaker give about makeup kits?

• Đáp án đúng: A
• Dấu hiệu: "which they’ll be expected to have with them at all times. Just the essentials will do". Have at all times = always carry, essentials = basic kit.

16. What advice does the speaker give about creating a portfolio?

• Đáp án đúng: C
• Dấu hiệu: "But remember you’ll need to get approval from the makeup designer in charge of the department". Get approval = get permission.

Questions 17-20

What ability is required for each of the following duties? Choose the correct letter, A, B, or C.

Abilities:
A. being well-organised B. being flexible C. working quickly

17. Prepping an actor

• Đáp án đúng: C (working quickly)
• Dấu hiệu: "step in and get this done without wasting any time, otherwise the makeup artist will get behind schedule". Without wasting time = working quickly.

18. Continuity

• Đáp án đúng: A (being well-organised)
• Dấu hiệu: "This information needs to be kept in good order as a reshoot can mean replicating makeup months later". Kept in good order = being well-organised.

19. General

• Đáp án đúng: B (being flexible)
• Dấu hiệu: "doing anything from getting the teas and coffees to putting on a wash. Having a positive attitude and being willing to do whatever is asked of you". Willing to do whatever = being flexible.

20. Applying makeup

• Đáp án đúng: C (working quickly)
• Dấu hiệu: "If there are dozens of extras involved you’ll need to keep up a swift pace and not spend too long on each person". Swift pace / not spend too long = working quickly.
